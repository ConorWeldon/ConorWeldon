## About Me

I'm a Dublin-based builder working across **AI, analytics, automation, and product innovation**.

Right now, I work in **analytics and transformation**, where I build data products, reporting systems, CRM improvements, dashboards, automation pipelines, and decision-support tools that connect strategy with execution.

Alongside that, I'm in **Year 3 of a PhD** focused on **sentiment analysis in AI, large language models, and emotion-like representations in intelligent systems**. My research sits at the intersection of **AI, human-AI interaction, interpretability, and affective computing**.

I'm also the founder of **Yvolution**, where I’m exploring ambitious ideas in **neurotechnology, prosthetics, EEG/EMG systems, medtech, and intelligent human-centered products**.

I’ve previously worked at **Microsoft** and in **software/mobile development**, and I still love building practical things just as much as I love researching them.
